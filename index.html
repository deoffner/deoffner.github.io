Teaching Professor & Director of Undergraduate Studies

Welcome. I am a Teaching Professor and the Director of Undergraduate Studies in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at Carnegie Mellon University.

My academic interests center on extremal combinatorics, graph theory (particularly hypercube graphs and pursuit-evasion games), and undergraduate mathematics education.

Research Focus

My research primary focuses on extremal graph theory, hypercube graph decompositions, polychromatic colorings, and pursuit-evasion games on graphs (such as Cops and Robbers). I also regularly mentor undergraduate research across discrete mathematics and applied modeling.
