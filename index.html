Research Focus

My research primary focuses on extremal graph theory, hypercube graph decompositions, polychromatic colorings, and pursuit-evasion games on graphs (such as Cops and Robbers)[cite: 28, 41, 44, 47, 72]. I also regularly mentor undergraduate research across discrete mathematics and applied modeling[cite: 104].
